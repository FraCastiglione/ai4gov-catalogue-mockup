Quality assurance

High-quality learning. Recognised credentials.

Aligned with ISO 21001

All extracurricular activities presented here have successfully passed a dedicated quality evaluation in line with the ISO 21001 standard for educational organisations.

Earn an AI4Gov-X Open Badge

By completing any of these courses and achieving a positive assessment, participants will earn an interoperable AI4Gov‑X Open Badge that formally recognises the skills and competencies acquired. These badges can be shared across professional platforms, supporting learners in showcasing their achievements within the international innovation and public‑sector ecosystems.
